Users can add, remove, and reweight stocks, ETFs, bonds, metals, commodities, cash, and FX exposure; the engine then recalculates hypothetical P&L, VaR/Expected Shortfall, rolling diagnostics, additive contributions, stresses, and a reproducible management report. The public snapshot is synthetic and labeled as implementation evidence rather than historical performance.
